Step-by-Step Planting Guide

Follow these six steps to establish and maintain your home garden.

01

Choose Your Plant

Select a plant suited to your space and available sunlight. Beginners can try pechay, basil, or mint.

02

Prepare the Pot

Choose a clean pot with drainage holes. You can reuse a clean container that is safe for growing plants.

03

Prepare the Soil

Fill the pot with suitable potting mix. Moisten it lightly and leave space at the top for watering.

04

Plant the Seeds

Follow the seed packet for planting depth and spacing. Place seedling roots gently in the soil and cover them.

05

Water the Plant

Water gently after planting. Check the soil regularly and avoid keeping it constantly soggy.

06

Provide Regular Care

Place the pot where the plant gets suitable light. Monitor its growth and provide nutrients when needed.
